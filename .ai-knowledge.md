## 🔑 Key Features

### Multi-Tenancy
- ✅ Organization-based routing: `/:slug/dashboard`
- ✅ Data isolation per organization
- ✅ Custom branding (12 color palettes)
- ✅ Slug validation with `useOrganizationSlug` hook

### RBAC
- ✅ 120 granular permissions across 19 categories
- ✅ 5 system roles: Super Admin, Admin, Doctor, Nurse, Receptionist
- ✅ Custom role creation
- ✅ Permission guards in frontend

### FHIR R4 Compliance
- ✅ Patient model with fhir_id, mrn
- ✅ AllergyIntolerance (severity, reaction, onset_date)
- ✅ MedicationStatement (dosage, frequency, prescribed_by)
- ✅ Condition (icd10_code, severity, status)

### Subscriptions
- ✅ LemonSqueezy integration
- ✅ 3 plans: Basic, Professional, Enterprise
- ✅ Webhook sync for subscription status
